# NEAR Monetization Contract

Revenue must be layered without making the core discovery/chat experience unusable.

## Free
- Nearby discovery
- Chat
- NOW
- Connection
- Ads where appropriate

## Premium
Potential entitlements:
- advanced discovery controls
- profile customization
- higher visibility/boosts
- ad-free experience where platform policy permits
- expanded history/features that do not expose private location data

## Ads
- Native placements should be visually distinct and clearly labeled.
- Frequency caps are mandatory.
- Do not expose raw location to advertisers.
- Audience matching happens through privacy-preserving platform/first-party signals.

## Affiliate
- Offer matching is contextual and consent-aware.
- Store click/conversion attribution without unnecessary personal data.
- Affiliate links are disclosed.

## Local business
- Sponsored nearby placements
- Coupons/offers
- Campaign analytics
- No sale of precise location history

## Revenue telemetry
Track impressions, clicks, conversions, subscription state and campaign outcomes with minimal data retention.
